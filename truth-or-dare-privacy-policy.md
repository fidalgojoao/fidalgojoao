# Truth or Dare — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 15 October 2026

Truth or Dare is designed to work locally on your device. The app stores player names, preferences, selected level, custom cards, and game progress only in local app storage so your group can resume later.

We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information. We do not use advertising, analytics, accounts, location, contacts, camera, microphone, nearby devices, or Bluetooth.

Google Play Billing is used only when you choose to buy the optional Premium Levels one-time product. Google processes payment information under its own privacy policy; this app does not receive or store payment card details.

You can delete local game information at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in the app and release notes.
