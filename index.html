About me

From real-time operations
to real-world software.

Before we get started, you can call me Johnny! 😁

I started learning C++ in Codecademy in August 2026. My goal is to learn enough to build AAA games, operating systems and emulators from scratch. I currently rely a lot on AI to help me write code and understand it.

Another one of my passions is Android Custom ROMs. Once I feel comfortable with C++, I’ll start learning Kotlin and try to build ROMs. For now, I use AI to help me get a head start.

Before transitioning into software development, I worked as a WFM Real Time Analyst, helping manage the workforce of a project with nearly 600 employees. I also built Microsoft Excel reports that showed operational KPIs and helped supervisors focus on the points that mattered most.

I used Excel formulas, Power Query and Microsoft Power Automate to automate as much work as possible — turning tasks that took hours of manual effort into minutes.
