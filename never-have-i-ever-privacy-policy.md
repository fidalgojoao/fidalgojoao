# Privacy Policy for Never Have I Ever

Never Have I Ever is a local party game. It does not require an account and does not operate an advertising, analytics, or remote gameplay server.

## Information stored on your device

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

- Player names entered by you.
- Player gender and interaction preferences.
- Specific-player selections and per-player comfort-zone settings.
- The selected game level, turn progress, and gameplay settings.
- Custom Truth and Dare cards created or imported by you.
- Appearance preferences, including theme and system-color choices.
- A protected Premium entitlement status and expiry time used to support temporary offline access.

This information is used only to provide the app's features and allow a game to continue between sessions. The developer does not receive this locally stored game information.

## Data collection and sharing

Never Have I Ever does not sell personal information. The developer does not use advertising or analytics SDKs and does not operate a server that collects gameplay information.

Never Have I Ever does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, or other nearby devices.

## Google Play Billing

When you view Premium plans, make a purchase, or restore purchases, the app uses Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the transaction and provides the app with product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase tokens needed to unlock, verify, and acknowledge a purchase.

The app does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Google handles payment and purchase records under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

After a successful online verification, the app may store a protected Premium entitlement status on your device for up to 30 days. This allows paid features to continue working temporarily when an internet connection is unavailable. The cached entitlement is protected using Android's security facilities and is refreshed or removed following later Google Play verification.

## Text-to-speech

Prompt narration is optional. If you enable it, the displayed prompt is passed to the text-to-speech engine configured on your Android device. Depending on the engine you use, speech may be generated on the device or processed according to that engine provider's privacy practices. Never Have I Ever does not separately record or retain narrated audio.

## Custom-card import and export

The app reads a custom-card file only after you select it through Android's system file picker. It writes an exported file only to the destination you choose. Exported files remain under your control and are not automatically uploaded by the app.

## Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Files exported outside the app must be deleted separately from their saved location.

Google Play may retain purchase records associated with your Google account under Google's own retention policies. Never Have I Ever does not provide user accounts, so there is no separate in-app account to delete.

## Security

Never Have I Ever keeps gameplay information in Android's local app storage. Premium entitlement caching uses Android security facilities designed to protect the local verification record. No storage method can guarantee absolute security, but the app limits the information it handles and does not transmit local gameplay data to a developer-operated server.

## Children

Never Have I Ever is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the current version will be provided in the app and on the app's published privacy-policy page.
